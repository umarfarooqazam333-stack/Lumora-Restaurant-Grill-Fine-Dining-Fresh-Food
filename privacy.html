Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: September 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Lumora Restaurant & Grill. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and handled when you use this website, contact us, make a reservation or place an order through our available communication channels.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on how you interact with our website, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Reservation date and number of guests
  • Special requests or messages you provide
  • Order information submitted through WhatsApp

3. How We Use Information

Information provided through the website may be used to:

  • Process reservation requests
  • Respond to customer inquiries
  • Process food order requests
  • Communicate with customers about their requests
  • Improve our website and customer experience

4. WhatsApp Communication

Some reservation and ordering features on this website use WhatsApp to send information directly to the restaurant's designated WhatsApp number.

When you choose to use WhatsApp, your information may also be subject to WhatsApp's own privacy practices and policies.

5. Cookies and Local Storage

This website may use basic browser technologies such as local storage to provide certain features, including the shopping cart.

Items added to the shopping cart may be stored locally in your browser so the cart can continue working during your visit.

6. Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services such as Google Maps and WhatsApp to provide location, communication and ordering functionality.

Third-party services may collect or process information according to their own privacy policies.

7. Information Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for purposes unrelated to providing the requested service.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time to reflect changes to the website, services or applicable requirements. Any updated version will be posted on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact Lumora Restaurant & Grill through the contact details provided on our website.
